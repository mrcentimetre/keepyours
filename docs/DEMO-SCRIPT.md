# Demo video script

Target ≈ 3 minutes (hard limit 5). Recorded on an iPhone, screen recording, one clip per scene; the waiting is cut in the edit.

## Before recording

- **Fresh vault**: sign out or reinstall → new passkey → Setup **60/40, 5 minutes**.
- **Second wallet** with test USDC from https://faucet.circle.com/ (Arbitrum Sepolia), to play the client.
- **Telegram** connected to @KeepYoursBot; **notifications allowed** in the app.
- A **Focus mode** that only allows Keep Yours and Telegram, so no personal notifications appear.
- Dark theme, Control Center screen recording.
- No user numbers in the video. Traction only as a caption, and only real numbers (PostHog, the waitlist sheet).

## Scenes

### 1 · The problem (0:00–0:20)
*Talking head, or captions over the waitlist page.*
> "If you're paid in crypto, you know this: the USDC lands, and a day later most of it is gone. Bills, trades, impulse buys at 2am. There's no tool that saves for you. Just willpower."

### 2 · The idea (0:20–0:35)
*The app's Home screen.*
> "Keep Yours is a savings layer on Arbitrum. Every payment splits itself, some to spend, some kept. What you keep waits before it can leave. And if a client pays late, you can take an advance against your own savings."

### 3 · Sign up in seconds (0:35–0:55)
*Open the installed app → Create passkey (Face ID) → Setup: slide to 60/40, pick 5 minutes → Create my vault.*
> "No seed phrase. One Face ID, and network fees are covered. I choose my split and my waiting period: 72 hours on mainnet, 5 minutes here so you can see it."

### 4 · Getting paid, the split happens by itself (0:55–1:30)
*Get paid → show QR and address → **lock the phone** → from the second wallet send 20 USDC → lock-screen notification "$20.00 received · $8.00 kept · $12.00 to spend" → open the app: Kept $8, To spend $12, Activity shows the payment.*
> "My client pays this address. I don't open anything. The payment splits itself on-chain, and I'm told the moment it lands, even with the app closed."

### 5 · Savings can't leave on impulse (1:30–2:05)
*Withdraw → $5 → review shows the 5-minute wait → slide → Face ID → countdown → the Telegram message "Withdrawal of $5.00 started… if this wasn't you, cancel it" → back to the app → Cancel withdrawal.*
> "If I want my savings, they wait first: long enough for the 2am urge to pass. And if someone ever got into my phone, I'd be alerted and could cancel. The contract enforces it, not my willpower."

*Optional: request again, cut 5 minutes, Send to my wallet. "After the wait, it's mine, any time."*

### 6 · Client paying late? Advance (2:05–2:35)
*Advance → "Available now" → pick $5 → fee timeline (free 30 days / 1.5% / 3%) → slide → Face ID → To spend goes up → another payment from the second wallet → Activity shows it repaid first.*
> "When a client is late, I can borrow up to half of what I've kept, free for 30 days. My next payment repays it automatically, before the split. My own savings are the collateral, so there's no lender to chase me."

### 7 · The rules protect me (2:35–2:50)
*Settings → Split → lower keep to 30% → amber "Waits 5 minutes before it applies… protects your savings if someone else gets into your phone" → close. Raise to 50% → green "Applies right away".*
> "Even my own settings follow the rule: saving more applies instantly, saving less has to wait."

### 8 · Trust and close (2:50–3:10)
*Arbiscan (verified contract), then Home.*
> "Each person gets their own vault contract. No shared pool, no admin key; nobody can move your savings but you. The contracts are verified and live on Arbitrum Sepolia, with 64 tests and invariant testing, and a $200 cap until they're audited. Mainnet is next. Keep Yours. Get paid. Keep yours."

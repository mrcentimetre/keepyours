# Brand

## Name

**Keep Yours.** Always the full name in writing, in the app, on the site and in the logo.

Never "Keep" on its own as the product name: "KEEP" is a live trademark for financial services in the US (Keep Financial Technologies) and the UK (Dorix Ltd), and Keep Network is an existing crypto token. People saying "use Keep" in conversation is fine and expected.

- Domain: **keepyours.xyz**
- X: **@keepyours**
- Repo: github.com/mrcentimetre/keepyours

## Tagline

**Get paid. Keep yours.**

Secondary line, for banners and the site: *savings for people paid in crypto · arbitrum*

## Logo

A mint green hexagonal shield with an arrow cut out of it, pointing up and to the right: the money that stays, and the part that moves on. Mint on near-black.

Rules:
- Keep clear space of at least half the logo's width on every side.
- Don't recolour it, add gradients, rotate it, or put it on a busy photo.
- Minimum size 24 px. It must still read as one shape.
- On light backgrounds, use the dark version of the mark.

## Colours

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Night | `#060E0A` | App background |
| Card | `#12211A` | Cards and surfaces |
| Keep green | `#16B862` | Primary buttons, the logo, accents |
| Mint | `#62E6A0` | Highlights, charts, the glow |
| Cooldown amber | `#F4B545` | Waiting states, countdowns |
| Block red | `#FF7070` | Blocked actions, errors |
| Muted | `#8CA497` | Secondary text |
| Ink | `#EAF5EF` | Primary text on dark |

Semantic use matters: amber always means "waiting", red always means "blocked". Don't use them decoratively.

## Type

- Display and headings: **Bricolage Grotesque** (bold)
- Body and UI: **IBM Plex Sans**
- Numbers and addresses: **IBM Plex Mono**, with tabular figures wherever digits line up

## Voice

- Plain words. "Savings", not "yield-bearing position". "Waiting period", not "time-lock".
- Say what happens next: "Cancel, keep my savings" beats "Confirm".
- Never promise returns. Yield is variable and is described that way.
- Never imply the money is insured or guaranteed. It isn't.
- Lowercase and casual on X; sentence case in the app.

## Things we don't do

- No countdown pressure, fake scarcity, or "only 3 spots left".
- No made-up user numbers.
- No padlock-and-shield stock imagery.
- No token talk. There is no token.
